---- Introduction to Pax Health ----

Pax Health is sponsored by Shoppers Statistics LLC.

This is an ERISA product with eligibility requirements.

There is no association associated with this plan.

There is no network associated with this plan. The plan operates independently without network restrictions.

---- Plan Options ----

Pax Health offers six plan levels to meet different needs and budgets: Pax Health Wellness provides basic wellness and preventive care coverage, Pax Health 1 offers entry-level coverage, Pax Health 2 provides enhanced coverage, Pax Health 3 offers mid-tier coverage, Pax Health 4 provides advanced coverage, and Pax Health 5 offers premium coverage.

It's important to explain to prospects that this plan has no association or network.

The plan operates independently.

---- Eligibility Requirements ----

This is an ERISA product with eligibility requirements.

Eligibility requirements will be included in the enrollment process.

Please refer to the plan documents for complete eligibility details.

It's important to note that eligibility requirements are part of the enrollment process, and applicants must meet all eligibility criteria to enroll in Pax Health plans.

---- Plan Highlights ----

Here are the key highlights you'll want to emphasize when presenting Pax Health.

Preventive Care Benefits are available on Pax Health Wellness.

Telemedicine provides unlimited zero dollar visits on Wellness, 1, 2, and 3.

Prescription Coverage varies by plan level.

Hospitalization Coverage is available on plans 1 through 5 with annual maximums.

Six Plan Levels allow you to match prospects from basic wellness to premium coverage.

There are No Network Restrictions as the plan operates independently.

---- Preventive Care & Wellness Benefits ----

Let's review the preventive care and wellness benefits across all plan levels.

The benefits table shows coverage details for Preventive Care, Annual Wellness Exams, Telemedicine, and Generic Prescriptions across all six plan levels.

Pax Health Wellness includes preventive care at zero dollars copay with unlimited visits. Pax Health Wellness also includes an annual wellness exam at twenty-five dollars copay, limited to one exam per year with a maximum of one hundred fifty dollars per exam. Pax Health 4 and 5 also include annual wellness exams at twenty-five dollars copay, limited to one visit per year with the same maximum.

Telemedicine is available at zero dollars consult fee with unlimited visits on Pax Health Wellness, Pax Health 1, Pax Health 2, and Pax Health 3.

Generic prescriptions are covered at zero dollars copay at participating pharmacies on Pax Health Wellness.

---- Primary Care & Specialist Visits ----

Now let's cover primary care and specialist visit benefits.

Pax Health 1, 2, and 3 include primary care visits at twenty-five dollars copay. Pax Health 1 allows 3 visits per year with a maximum of one hundred fifty dollars per visit. Pax Health 2 allows 4 visits per year with the same maximum. Pax Health 3 also allows 4 visits per year.

Pax Health 4 and 5 include primary care visits at fifty dollars copay. Pax Health 4 allows 4 visits per year, while Pax Health 5 allows 5 visits per year, both with a maximum of one hundred fifty dollars per visit.

Specialist or urgent care visits are available on Pax Health 1, 2, and 3 at fifty dollars copay. Pax Health 1 allows 1 visit per year with a maximum of three hundred dollars per visit. Pax Health 2 allows 2 visits per year, and Pax Health 3 allows 4 visits per year, both with the same three hundred dollar maximum per visit.

On Pax Health 4 and 5, specialist or urgent care is covered at seventy-five dollars per day. Pax Health 4 allows 4 visits per year, while Pax Health 5 allows 5 visits per year, both with a maximum of three hundred dollars per visit.

---- Hospitalization & Surgery ----

Let's discuss hospitalization and surgery benefits.

The benefits table shows hospitalization and surgery coverage details across the plan levels.

Hospitalization is covered on Pax Health 1 through 5 at one thousand dollars per day, except Pax Health 5 which is one thousand five hundred dollars per day. Pax Health 1 has an annual maximum of up to five thousand dollars per year. Pax Health 2 and 4 have an annual maximum of up to ten thousand dollars per year. Pax Health 3 and 5 have an annual maximum of up to fifteen thousand dollars per year. All hospitalization benefits are subject to a twelve-month pre-existing condition limitation.

Surgery is available on Pax Health 4 and 5. Pax Health 4 covers surgery at one thousand dollars per year with an annual maximum of up to two thousand dollars. Pax Health 5 covers surgery at one thousand five hundred dollars per year with an annual maximum of up to four thousand five hundred dollars.

---- Emergency Services ----

Emergency services are available on Pax Health 4 and 5.

Emergency room visits, if admitted, are covered at one thousand dollars per incident.

Ambulance services, if admitted, are covered at five hundred dollars per incident.

Emergency services are available on Pax Health 4 and 5 only.

---- Prescription Coverage ----

Let's review prescription coverage across the plans.

Pax Health Wellness covers generic prescriptions at zero dollars copay at participating pharmacies. Pax Health 1 offers discount prescriptions only with no brand coverage.

Pax Health 2 covers generic prescriptions at zero dollars copay and preferred prescriptions at five dollars copay, with a prescription assistance program available for specialty drugs.

Pax Health 3 covers generic prescriptions at zero dollars copay, preferred prescriptions at five dollars copay, non-preferred generic at five to ten dollars retail, and brand prescriptions at forty dollars retail, with a one hundred fifty dollar monthly prescription limit per person.

Pax Health 4 and 5 both cover generic prescriptions at zero dollars copay, preferred generic prescriptions at five dollars copay, and brand prescription coverage at forty dollars retail, with a one hundred fifty dollar monthly prescription limit per person. Specialty drugs are excluded on all plans, and pre-existing conditions are excluded for twelve months for hospitalization.

---- Exclusions - Pax Health Wellness ----

It's important to understand what Pax Health Wellness does not cover.

Hospital services are not covered.

Surgery is not covered.

Specialty drugs are not covered.

Maternity services are not covered.

Other services not listed in the schedule of benefits are not covered.

---- Limitations and Exclusions ----

All Pax Health plans have important limitations and exclusions that agents must clearly explain.

Pre-existing conditions are excluded for twelve months for hospitalization on all plans. This means any condition that existed before enrollment will not be covered for hospitalization benefits during the first twelve months of coverage.

Specialty drugs are excluded on all plans. This is an important limitation to communicate to prospects.

Additional exclusions include hospital services, surgery, specialty drugs, maternity, and other services not listed in the schedule of benefits. Pre-existing conditions are excluded for twelve months for hospitalization, and other exclusions apply per the plan document. Always direct members to the plan documents for complete limitations and exclusions.
