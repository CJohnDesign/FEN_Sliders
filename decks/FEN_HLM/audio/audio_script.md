---- Introduction to Heirloom ----

Heirloom, underwritten by ManhattanLife Insurance and Annuity Company,

is an EMA membership package which includes a variety of discount benefits, as well as Group Term with Accelerated Death Benefit plan underwritten by ManhattanLife Insurance and Annuity Company.

When presenting to prospects, emphasize how the insured benefits provide financial protection with life insurance that can be accessed during terminal illness, combined with accidental death and dismemberment benefits including seatbelt, coma, occupational assault, and paralysis coverage.

---- Key Features and Benefits to Group Life Benefits ----

Let's explore the key group life benefit features you'll be highlighting to prospects.

Level Term Life Insurance is renewable to age eighty and available in ten-year term durations, providing long-term protection for your clients.

Four Plan Options are available with five thousand, ten thousand, fifteen thousand, or twenty thousand dollars in base life benefit, allowing members to choose coverage that fits their needs and budget.

The Accelerated Death Benefit provides up to fifty percent of the base policy for terminal illness when the insured has twelve months or less to live.

AD&D Coverage matches the base life benefit amount and includes additional riders for seatbelt, coma, occupational assault, and paralysis benefits.

Portability is available after six months of continuous coverage if the insured is under age seventy, not totally disabled, and no longer actively at work for the association.

The Conversion Right allows members to convert to a Whole Life policy without evidence of insurability after five years of coverage, as long as they are younger than sixty-five.

Group Life Benefit Waiting Period is important to communicate. Term Life Insurance Benefits do not go into effect until the first of the month following eleven months of continuous paid membership.

Now let's review the plan benefit levels options.

---- Group Life Features ----

Let's examine the additional group life features you need to understand and communicate to prospects.

The Accelerated Benefit for Terminal Illness accelerates payment of the life insurance death benefit if the insured is diagnosed as terminally ill with twelve months or less to live, or experiences loss from specified critical illnesses. The maximum advance is fifty percent of the base policy. This benefit is subject to Pre-existing Condition Limitations.

Portability is available after six months of continuous coverage if the group master policy remains in force and the insured is less than age seventy, not Totally Disabled, and no longer Actively at work for the Association. Members continue paying premiums via direct billing. Dependents on ported certificates terminate when the spouse reaches age seventy or the child reaches age twenty-five.

The Conversion feature allows the policy to be converted to a Whole Life plan without evidence of insurability if the insured is younger than sixty-five and has been insured for at least five years. Spouse coverage can be converted before age sixty-five, and child coverage can be converted before age twenty-six with limits up to three times the benefit amount, maximum twenty-five thousand dollars.

---- Group Life Insurance Pre-existing Condition Limitations ----

It's important to understand the pre-existing condition limitations for the insured benefits so you can properly set expectations with prospects.

If a member has a pre-existing condition that was diagnosed or had symptoms occurring in the twelve months prior to the policy effective date, no benefits will be paid for the first twelve months of coverage. This limitation applies specifically to the Terminal Illness Benefit and Waiver of Premium benefits. Members should refer to their Certificate of coverage for the complete list of specific pre-existing conditions.

---- Accidental Death, Loss of Sight and Dismemberment Benefit - Limitations and Exclusions ----

As agents, you need to understand the key limitations for Accidental Death, Loss of Sight and Dismemberment Benefits to properly set expectations with your prospects.

The loss must occur within one hundred eighty days after the Accident. The benefit terminates for the insured when a Loss of Sight or Dismemberment Benefit is paid.

If two or more losses result from one Accident, only one benefit will be paid, which will be the largest amount available for any one of the losses. This limitation does not apply to Coma, Occupational Assault, Paralysis, or Seatbelt Benefits.

If two or more Accidents cause losses covered by this benefit, the total payment will not exceed one hundred percent of the Accidental Death Benefit for all such losses combined. Again, this does not apply to Coma, Occupational Assault, Paralysis, or Seatbelt Benefits.

No benefit is payable if the loss was caused directly or indirectly, wholly or partly, from suicide or attempted suicide, whether the insured was sane or insane.

---- Group Term Life Exclusions ----

Now let's review the exclusions for Group Term Life Benefits that you need to communicate to prospects.

Key exclusions include any intentionally self-inflicted injury, and injury intentionally inflicted by any person, except this does not apply to Occupational Assault or when the insured is an innocent bystander having no relationship to an altercation.

Additional exclusions include substance abuse, though this does not exclude losses brought about by use of drugs prescribed by and used as directed by a Physician. War or act of war, whether declared or undeclared, is also excluded.

Service in the armed forces of any country or organization, bacterial infection unless caused by an Accident, committing or attempting to commit an assault or felony, and resisting or fleeing from arrest are all excluded.

The policy also excludes participating in a riot or insurrection, voluntarily taking or absorbing poison or poisonous gas or fumes, traveling in any kind of aircraft unless a fare-paying passenger on a regularly scheduled flight, intoxication, and racing a self-propelled vehicle on a racetrack, public road, or other place.

Remember, exclusions and limitations vary by state, so members should always refer to the Certificate or Master Policy for full terms and conditions of coverage.
