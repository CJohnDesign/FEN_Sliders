## Group Life Features

<v-click>

**Accelerated Benefit for Terminal Illness**

Accelerates payment of the life insurance death benefit in the event of a terminal illness diagnosis (12 months or less to live) or loss from specified critical illnesses. Maximum advance is **50% of the base policy**. Subject to Pre-existing Condition Limitations.

</v-click>

<v-click>

**Portability**

Portable after **6 months** of continuous coverage if the group master policy remains in force and the insured is **less than age 70**, not Totally Disabled, and no longer Actively at work for the Association. Premiums continue via direct billing. Dependents on ported certificates terminate when spouse reaches age 70 or child reaches age 25.

</v-click>

<v-click>

**Conversion**

The policy can be converted **without evidence of insurability** if the insured is younger than 65 and has been insured for at least **5 years**. Converts to Whole Life plan. Spouse coverage can be converted before age 65; child coverage before age 26 (up to 3x benefit amount, max $25,000).

</v-click>

<small>

**Group Life Benefit Waiting Period:** Term Life Insurance Benefits do not go into effect until the 1st of the month following 11 months of continuous paid Membership.

**Coverage Type:** Level term life insurance is renewable to age 80 and available in term durations of 10 years.

## Group Life Insurance Pre-Existing Conditions Limitations

<small>

Understanding pre-existing condition limitations helps you properly set expectations with prospects.

</small>

<v-click>

**Pre-existing Condition Definition:**

If a member has a pre-existing condition that is **diagnosed or symptoms occurred** in the **12 months prior** to the policy effective date, **no benefits will be paid** for the first **12 months** of the policy effective date.

This limitation applies to:
- **Terminal Illness Benefit**
- **Waiver of Premium Benefit**

Refer to the Certificate of coverage for specific pre-existing conditions.

</v-click>

<small>

**Disclaimer:** Exclusions and limitations vary by state and apply solely to the insured benefit included in the Heirloom Package. Please refer to the applicable Certificate or Master Policy for complete terms and conditions of coverage, including effective dates, benefits, limitations, and exclusions related to the insured benefit.

## Accidental Death, Loss of Sight and Dismemberment Benefit - Limitations and Exclusions

<small>

**Key limitations for Accidental Death, Loss of Sight and Dismemberment Benefits:**

</small>

<v-click>

- Loss must occur within **180 days** after the Accident
- Benefit terminates when a Loss of Sight or Dismemberment Benefit is paid
</v-click>

<v-click>

- If **two or more losses** result from one Accident, only **one benefit** (the largest) is paid
- This does not apply to: Coma, Occupational Assault, Paralysis, or Seatbelt Benefits
</v-click>

<v-click>

- If **two or more Accidents** cause losses, payment will not exceed **100% of Accidental Death Benefit** combined
- This does not apply to: Coma, Occupational Assault, Paralysis, or Seatbelt Benefits
</v-click>

<v-click>

- **No benefit payable** if loss was caused directly or indirectly by **suicide or attempted suicide**, whether sane or insane
</v-click>

<small>

**Disclaimer:** Exclusions and limitations vary by state and apply solely to the insured benefit included in the Heirloom Package. Please refer to the applicable Certificate or Master Policy for complete terms and conditions of coverage, including effective dates, benefits, limitations, and exclusions related to the insured benefit.

</small>
